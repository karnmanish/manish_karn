FEATURED RESEARCH

A direct-current MPPT loop for grid-interfaced solar inverters

My M.Tech work analysed ground current to reduce Potential Induced Degradation in PV modules — research that fed directly into a published control method for eliminating the outer loop in grid-tied solar inverter MPPT design.

My journey

My path runs from undergraduate work on illumination and solar feasibility at Kathmandu University, through graduate research on grid-tied solar inverters at IIT Ropar, to hands-on industrial automation — PLC, SCADA and HMI systems on live hydropower plants.

Since 2023 I've brought that same range into the classroom, teaching circuits, signals and power systems to the next generation of engineers.
